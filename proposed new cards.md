# Proposed New Cards (Foundry Set)

All new cards have **draw 0**.

## 1) Prospector
- **Trade:** Prospector + Bronze → **dig until non‑bronze** (discard bronzes, add first non‑bronze to hand).
- Purpose: platinum‑like effect with cheaper cost but narrower filter.

## 2) Alloy
- **Static:** In Archive, Alloy counts as **1 Bronze AND 1 Silver** for trade costs.
- Purpose: makes platinum/hallmark‑style trades easier without adding draw.

## 3) Assayer
- **Trade:** Assayer + any non‑gold → **tutor Prospector or Alloy** (shuffle).
- Purpose: supports the two core efficiency pieces.

## 4) Smelter
- **Trade:** Smelter + **3 Bronze** → **tutor 1 Silver** (shuffle).
- Purpose: efficiency discount for bronze→silver trades.

## 5) Refiner
- **Trade:** Refiner + **3 Silver** → **tutor 1 Gold** (shuffle).
- Purpose: efficiency discount for silver→gold trades.

## Balance Knobs (optional)
- Change Smelter/Refiner costs to **4 Bronze / 4 Silver**.
- Or make Refiner reward **archive** a gold instead of **hand**.
- Or make Assayer tutor **only Alloy** (not Prospector).
