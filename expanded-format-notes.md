# Expanded Format Notes

## Formats
- Core only (starter format)
- Expanded (player-built, fixed 180-card decks)

## Expanded Deck Rules
- Fixed deck size: 180 cards
- Core base is always included:
  - 125 bronze
  - 25 silver
  - 5 gold
- Plus 5 copies of 5 chosen non-core cards

## Unlock Model
- Gilded Gems unlocks first as a full pack so players can play Expanded immediately.
- After that, individual cards unlock over time.

## Expanded Pool
- All non-core cards are eligible as they are unlocked:
  - Gilded Gems
  - Ancients
  - Minted
  - Future expansions

## Future Decisions (not finalized)
- Expanded deck selection UI (pick 5 non-core cards).
- Persistence for unlocks (local storage vs server).
- Online enforcement of deck selection.
- CPU deck selection strategy (mirror player or default).
